Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Questions

How do I put a countdown on my Lock Screen?
  1. In Love Lock, tap +, pick a preset, fill in the date, and tap Save.
  2. Lock your iPhone, then touch and hold the Lock Screen and tap Customize, then Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the widget area below the clock (or the line above it, for an inline widget), choose Love Lock, and add the widget whose number matches your lock.
Can I put a countdown on my Home Screen?

Yes. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Choose Love Lock, swipe to the small or medium size, and add the widget whose number matches your lock. The small size also works in StandBy, when your iPhone is charging on its side.

Why are the widgets called Lock 1, Lock 2, and so on?

iOS can't list widgets named after things you create in an app, so Love Lock offers eight numbered widgets. Lock 1 shows the first lock in the app, Lock 2 the second, and so on. Each lock in the app is labeled with its widget, such as "Widget: Lock 2". Reordering your locks in the app (tap Edit, then drag) changes which lock each widget shows.

My widget says "Add a lock".

That widget's number is higher than the number of locks you have. For example, a Lock 3 widget with only two locks in the app. Add another lock, or swap the widget for one with a lower number. Only the first eight locks have widgets.

Love Lock doesn't appear in the widget list.

Open the Love Lock app once after installing it, then try adding the widget again. If it's still missing, restart your iPhone. iOS sometimes needs a restart to refresh its list of widgets after an app is installed or updated.

How do Rotate and Next up work?

When a lock has more than one countdown, Rotate shows them in turn, in the order you set, every 15 minutes, hour, 6 hours, or day. Next up always shows whichever countdown is closest, which suits holidays or upcoming days off. The US Holidays preset group uses Next up.

What happens when I travel to another time zone?

By default, countdowns follow you: a date ends at midnight wherever you are. To count to an exact moment in one place, such as a birth time, open the countdown, and turn on Pin to a time zone. Pinned countdowns don't move when you travel.

Why does my countdown still say "today"?

Some days last longer than one day. Hanukkah and Passover show "today" for 8 days, and Rosh Hashanah for 2. Any countdown can set how many days it lasts under Lasts, so a week-long vacation shows "today" until it's over and then counts up. Jewish holidays count to their first full day; they begin at sundown the evening before.

How do I delete a lock or a countdown?

Swipe it to the left and tap Delete. A lock always keeps at least one countdown. Deleting a lock moves every lock after it up one widget number.

For parents

Love Lock is rated 4+ on the App Store. It's a private countdown app, with nothing in it that connects a child to other people or to the internet.

  • No accounts, chat, or sharing. Nothing typed into Love Lock is seen by anyone else.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases.
  • No web browsing. The app has no links out to websites and doesn't connect to the internet.
  • Everything stays on the device. Love Lock collects no data. See the privacy policy.

The only content is what someone types in: names, dates, and emoji for their countdowns. These appear in widgets on the Lock Screen and Home Screen, where anyone holding the phone can see them.
