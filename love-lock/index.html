Lock Screen widgets for iPhone

The dates that matter, every time you look.

Love Lock counts down to, or up from, anniversaries, birthdays, due dates, vacations and holidays, right on your Lock Screen. Put several in one widget and it rotates through them, or always shows whichever is next.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone with iOS 18 or later. No account, no ads, and nothing leaves your phone.

Made for the Lock Screen

Each lock in the app is one Lock Screen widget. Fill it with one countdown or a handful, and choose how it shows them.

🔄

Rotate or Next up

A lock with several countdowns rotates through them every 15 minutes, hour, 6 hours or day. Or set it to Next up, and it always shows whichever date is closest.

📐

Every Lock Screen size

Circular, rectangular and inline widgets. Make up to eight locks, each with its own widget.

⏱️

Your choice of units

Years, months, days, hours and minutes, in any combination: "3 months, 12 days" or just "104 days".

💍

Since or until

Yearly dates can count up from the first one ("6 years together") or down to the next, with a "Today! 🎉" on the day itself.

📅

Dates that move

Use a rule like "fourth Thursday of November" or "last Monday of May", and it's worked out again every year. Holidays can follow Easter, or the Hebrew or Chinese calendar.

🏖️

Events that last

A week-long trip shows "today" for the whole week, then counts up. Hanukkah and Passover last 8 days, Rosh Hashanah 2.

🌍

Time zones

Dates follow you when you travel, so midnight is your midnight. Pin a countdown to one time zone when the exact moment matters, such as a birth time.

🔋

Easy on your battery

iOS keeps the countdown text current on its own. The widget barely wakes: at most once a day, and only for locks that need it.

🔐

Private

Your dates stay on your iPhone. No account, no network connection, no analytics, no tracking. Privacy policy

In the app

Start a lock from a preset, add countdowns, and see exactly what your Lock Screen will show before you save.

Presets for the big days

Every preset comes with sensible units and its date worked out for you. The ready-made US Holidays lock shows whichever of ten days off and family holidays comes next.

Add it to your Lock Screen

  1. In Love Lock, tap +, pick a preset, and fill in the date.
  2. Optionally, tap Add Countdown for more dates, then choose Rotate or Next up.
  3. On your Lock Screen, touch and hold, tap Customize, then Lock Screen. Tap the widget area, choose Love Lock, and add the widget whose number matches your lock, such as Lock 1.

Widgets are numbered because iOS can't name them after your own locks. Lock 1 shows the first lock in the app, Lock 2 the second, and so on. More in Support
