Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Love Lock collects no data. Your countdowns stay on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What Love Lock stores

Love Lock saves what you enter (each lock's name, its countdowns' titles, emoji, dates and settings) on your iPhone, in storage shared only by the Love Lock app and its own widgets. It also remembers the time zone your iPhone was last in, so it can update your widgets after you travel.

This information never leaves your device through Love Lock.

What Love Lock doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No network connections. The app and widget don't connect to the internet.
  • No analytics, crash reporting SDKs, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, calendar, location, or health data.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

Backups

Like other app data, your locks are included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup to your computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and covered by Apple's privacy policy, not ours. We can't see them.

Deleting your data

Delete a lock by swiping it to the left in the app. Delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Love Lock collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Love Lock ever began collecting data, the policy would be updated before that version of the app was released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See Support for how to reach us.
