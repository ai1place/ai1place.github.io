Menu-bar app for macOS

Every AI coding session, at a glance.

Advisor follows your Claude Code, Codex, Copilot, Cursor, and Grok Build sessions and shows what each one is doing. When any of them needs you, the robot in your menu bar says how many, and one click brings that window forward.

Right in your menu bar

The robot takes the color of whatever is most urgent across your sessions. When some are waiting on you, it shows how many in its visor and blinks while they wait.

  • Every session, one click away. Open the menu to see them all, each with its state's color, then pick one to bring its window forward.
  • Or skip the click. ⌃⌥0 opens the menu from any app, and ⌃⌥1 through ⌃⌥9 bring a session's window forward. The menu shows which number is which.
  • Quiet when nothing is. With no sessions running, the robot dozes off.
  • QuietNo sessions running.
  • ThinkingAn agent is working on your prompt.
  • Tool runningA command, edit, or search is underway.
  • Needs inputTwo sessions are waiting on you.

One color per state

Each state has one color in the window, the menu, and the menu bar. Needs input and a stalled tool blink, so they stand out from the solid color next to them.

In the app

Open Advisor for the full picture: every session, what it was asked, and what it's doing right now.

Grouped by agent

Sessions sit together by agent, each numbered, with its state and how long it has been in it.

The prompt at a glance

Each session shows the first line of its latest prompt. Turn on local AI summaries for a one-line title, written on your Mac.

Live tool activity

See which tool is running and for how long, like "Running Bash: cargo test". A call that goes quiet is flagged "no response yet".

Click to focus

Click a session to bring its window forward, whether it's in Terminal, iTerm2, tmux, VS Code, IntelliJ, or Zed.

Your order

Click a session's number to move it. It swaps places with whichever session had that number.

Nothing to set up per session

New sessions appear on their own as soon as an agent starts, and finished ones leave when they end.

Get started

  1. Open the disk image and drag Advisor to Applications.
  2. Open Advisor. Grant Accessibility and Automation when macOS asks. Bringing the right window forward needs them.
  3. Open the Agents view. It lists each coding agent it finds and shows the exact hook change. Nothing is written until you apply it.

Have a QMK keyboard?

Advisor can also light one key per session, in the same colors, and pressing that key brings its window forward. It takes custom QMK firmware, with keymaps ready for the NuPhy Air75 V2 and the Keychron K0 Max, and a USB-C cable that carries data. None of it is needed for anything above.
